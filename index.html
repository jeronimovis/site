Your guide & CVCórdoba · Santiago del Estero · Online

20+ years helping people speak up in English

Córdoba, Santiago del Estero, now online

I've met many capable students who go quiet in English, not because they don't know enough, but because no one gave them the chance to use it. Getting them talking is my job.

I have a degree in English teaching. At Facultad de Lenguas, UNC, I taught intensive courses and later trained other teachers to work in virtual classrooms. Then came institutes and secondary schools, and in 2021 I moved to Zoom® full-time. Today I teach adults and professionals from all over Argentina.
