## Warm-up | Pairs · 2 minutes
> Talk with a partner.

[discuss] What do you like to do on a free morning?

## Key words | Individual · 2 minutes
> Match the words with the meanings.

[match]
suggest = say "we can do this"
rent = pay money to use something for a short time
interview = a meeting with questions and answers
artist = a person who paints or makes art
details = small pieces of information
map = a picture that shows streets and places
[/match]

# Part 3 – Language and speaking

## Can and let's | Individual · 2 minutes
> Choose the correct word.

We {*can|can't} rent bikes. It's easy.

Sorry, he {*can|can't} only do this morning.

Jenny has a map, so she {*can|can't} find the Tate Modern.

Let's {*meet|meeting|to meet} at 12 o'clock.

## Your free morning | Pairs · 2 minutes
> A friend visits your town. Make a plan for a free morning. Use the words in the box.

[phrases "Useful language"]
What do you want to do?
We can go…
We can't… because…
Let's meet at… o'clock.
That's cool! / Great!
[/phrases]

[discuss] What can you and your friend do in your town on a free morning?

[discuss] Where and when can you meet?

[teacher]
**Video:** “English File 4thE - Elementary - Practical English E4 - Getting lost - A free morning”, channel Gina Sciangula. The intro title card (00:00–00:15) and the fade-out are skipped.

**Clip:** 00:15–01:21 (one clip, played twice). Rob and Jenny plan to rent bikes and cycle through London's parks; Daniel calls and Rob must interview an artist at the Tate Modern; Rob and Jenny agree to meet outside the Tate Modern at 12 o'clock.

**Timing (15 min):** warm-up 2 · key words 2 · first watch 3 · second watch 4 · can/let's 2 · speaking 2.

**Grammar in the clip:**
- *can* for possibility and suggestions: "Well, we can go cycling." (00:25) · "We can rent bikes." (00:28)
- *can* for ability: "I can find it. I have a map. I can cycle there." (01:14)
- *let's* for proposals: "Let's meet at 12 o'clock then." (01:18)
- Also useful: "I don't have a bike." (00:27) and "You need to do an interview…" (00:47)

**If time is short:** skip the warm-up prediction question, or do the can/let's exercise orally as a class.

**Fast finishers / homework:** write a short dialogue (4–6 lines) to invite a friend to do something on Saturday, using *can*, *can't* and *let's*. Optional extra discussion: Jenny says "Work is work." Is school sometimes more important than free time?

**Note:** the gap fill uses the exact words of the clip; the transcript is folded so students open it only after checking.
